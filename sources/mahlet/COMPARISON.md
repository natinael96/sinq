# Book-first Mahlet comparison

This is a conservative editorial edition, not a claim of liturgical approval.

- Book: 61 observances, 100 orders; all 4071 paragraphs retained.
- Telegram: 36 matched feasts; 48 additional dated feasts.
- Retained 347 distinct candidate versions, including 5 versions supplying a service absent in the matched book feast.
- Consolidated 11 typography-equivalent editions with their source links.
- Review: 41 feast records (85 orders), plus 52 near-repeated editions.

## Important differences

- No Yekatit chapter exists in the supplied book. Dated Telegram material supplies that month; undated records remain in review.
- Tir Giorgis: book Mahlet says 18; Telegram says 23. These were not merged.
- Ginbot Tekle Haymanot / Kristos Samra: book prints ፲ /፪/; Telegram says 12. No silent date correction.
- Tikimt Medhanealem: book Mahlet has no day; Telegram supplies 27. Source attribution is explicit. Wazema’s printed 26 remains intact.
- Megabit Medhanealem: Telegram supplies day 27, absent from the book heading.
- Combined and single-saint feasts remain distinct; partial overlap is not identity.
- Seasonal Sundays, Holy Week, Easter, Ascension and Pentecost retain book placement but have no fixed calendar day. Printed headings are not perpetual calendar rules.
- Unclosed feast names, unknown dates, and book alternative scopes are preserved for review.
