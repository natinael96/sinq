Privacy Policy

App: Sinq (ስንቅ), package com.agpeya.app · Effective 31 August 2026

The short version

Sinq collects no data. None.

The app has no internet permission: it is technically incapable of sending anything off your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data SDKs of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in Sinq is stored locally, on your device only:

  • Your settings: language, theme, reading preferences, reminder times.
  • Your ጉዞ record: which hours you marked as prayed, and your habits.
  • Your bookmarks, highlights, prayer list and custom prayer hours.
  • Your ማስታወሻ journal entries, and their passphrase if you set one.
  • Your አስራት ledger and ስዕለት vows, including any amounts you enter.

None of this leaves your device. Android's automatic cloud backup is deliberately disabled for this app, so your prayer records are not uploaded to Google either.

The journal, and ንስሐ notes

Journal entries can be locked behind a passphrase you choose. While the journal is open it is excluded from screenshots and from the app switcher preview, so it does not appear in a thumbnail someone else might see.

Notes written to prepare for ንስሐ are built to be destroyed. They never enter a backup file, and marking the confession as made deletes the note rather than filing it away.

The passphrase protects the journal from someone holding your unlocked phone. It is not full-disk encryption of the underlying database, and it does not defend against an attacker with physical access and technical means.

Backups you create yourself

Sinq can export your data as a file when you ask it to (Settings → Data). Export is a checklist: you choose what goes in, the journal is excluded by default, and including it requires your passphrase.

The exported file is plain text, written to the location you choose. The app never transmits it anywhere, but once it exists, what happens to it is yours to look after. Restoring reads a file you pick.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications: to show the prayer reminders and alarms you set. Asked only when you enable a reminder.
  • Exact alarms: so a prayer alarm rings at the exact time you chose.
  • Run at startup: to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration: for the alarm's vibration pattern.

There is no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage-scanning permission.

Children

Sinq is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sinq ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated first, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The effective date at the top always reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Sinq: open an issue at github.com/natinael96/sinq/issues.
