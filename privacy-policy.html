ስንቅ · Sinq — Privacy Policy

App: Sinq (package com.agpeya.app) · Effective 31 August 2026

The short version

Sinq collects no data. None. The app has no internet permission — it is technically incapable of sending anything off your device. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party data SDKs of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in Sinq is stored locally on your device only:

  • Your settings (language, theme, reading preferences, reminder times).
  • Your prayer journal: which hours you marked as prayed, streaks, and habits.
  • Your bookmarks, highlights, prayer list, and custom prayer hours.

None of this leaves your device. Android's automatic cloud backup is deliberately disabled for this app, so your prayer records are not uploaded to Google either.

Backups you create yourself

Sinq can export your data as a file when you ask it to (Settings → Data). That file is written to the location you choose and stays under your control; the app never transmits it anywhere. Restoring reads a file you pick. What you do with the exported file is up to you.

Permissions the app asks for, and why

  • Notifications — to show the prayer reminders and alarms you set. Asked only when you enable a reminder.
  • Exact alarms — so a prayer alarm rings at the exact time you chose.
  • Run at startup — to re-arm your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Vibration — for the alarm's vibration pattern.

There is no internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage-scanning permission.

Children

Sinq is suitable for all ages. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Sinq ever changes any of the above, this page will be updated first, and the change will be described in the app's release notes. The policy's effective date at the top always reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Sinq: open an issue at github.com/natinael96/sinq/issues, or use the support contact listed on the app's Play Store page.
