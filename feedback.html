Send feedback

A wrong reading on a day, a translation that reads badly, a commemoration that is missing, an idea, or something that simply broke. Corrections to the prayer text and the Amharic are the most useful thing anyone can send.

What is this about?
Write in Amharic or English, whichever is easier. If it is a correction, saying what the text should be is worth more than saying it is wrong.
The screen or the day it happened on, for example “ግጻዌ, ሐምሌ ፯” or “the Psalter, መዝሙር ፻፲፰”.
Only if you want an answer. Nothing is sent to you otherwise, and it is never shown to anyone else.

What happens to it

The form sends what you typed and nothing else. No cookie is set, your address is not logged, and the page carries no analytics, so reading this page leaves no trace at all. What arrives is your message, the kind you picked, the screen you named, the app version, and your email if you chose to give one.

This is about the website, not the app. Sinq itself sends nothing about you: it reaches the network only to check once a day for a new version, which you can switch off, and to open a Catena page when you tap one. See the privacy policy.

Other ways

If you have a GitHub account, an issue is better for anything that needs a conversation, because you get replies and can see what was done about it. The form is here for everyone who does not.
