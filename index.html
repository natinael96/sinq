What it is

An app for praying the hours, not for reading about them.

Sinq holds the prayers a day is built from, ጸሎተ ነግህ at dawn through መንፈቀ ሌሊት at night, and opens on whichever one the hour suggests. Around them sit the readings that place the day in the Church's year: the ግጻዌ appointed for the date, the ስንክሳር commemoration with its አርኬ, and whichever fast is in effect.

The interface is Amharic first and English if you prefer it. Everything is set in Ethiopic type chosen for reading at length, on a deep liturgical green with gold marking what matters.

Offline by construction

Sinq has no internet permission. This is not a promise not to collect data: the app is built without the ability to reach a network at all. Every psalm, every reading, all 366 days of the lectionary are inside the file before you install it.

It works on a phone with no SIM, in a basement, on a plane, on a bundle that ran out. Nothing you mark, write or highlight leaves the device, and Android's automatic cloud backup is deliberately switched off for this app, so your prayer records don't reach Google either.

Inside the app

ጸሎት Prayer

  • The seven hours ጸሎተ ነግህ, ሠለስት, ቀትር, ተሰዓት, ሰርክ, ንዋም and መንፈቀ ሌሊት with its three watches, plus the Veil prayer. The home screen suggests the hour the time of day calls for.
  • ውዳሴ ማርያም and ጸሎት ዘዘወትር A portion for each weekday, with ይወድስዋ መላእክት and አንቀጸ ብርሃን, in Amharic, with a Ge'ez toggle.
  • Hours you shape yourself Build a custom hour from the psalms and passages you actually pray, and keep a prayer list of your own.

የቀን መቁጠሪያ Calendar and lectionary

  • ግጻዌ, the complete lectionary All 366 fixed dates, the movable weekday seasons and the Sunday and mezmur cycle, resolved for the year through the ባሕረ ሓሳብ. Every citation opens in the reader.
  • ስንክሳር The day's commemorations in Amharic, with its አርኬ hymn and the fixed closing prayer.
  • አጽዋማት What fast is in effect today, and every fast of the Ethiopian year.
  • The ባሕረ ሓሳብ table The printed 2001–2015 EC annual table, from the Library.

ንባብ Reading

  • The whole Bible, bundled The Amharic 1980 Ethiopian Orthodox canon, Old and New Testament, with no connection needed.
  • All 150 psalms Amharic by default with a Ge'ez 1980 switch in the reader, numbered as the Ge'ez Psalter numbers them.
  • Type that suits your eyes Scroll or turn page by page; five text sizes, four Ethiopic faces, three line spacings and four alignments, matched across every reading surface. The screen can stay awake while you pray, and each hour remembers where you stopped.

የግል Yours

  • ማስታወሻ, a journal that knows the day Write the day down, browsed a ግእዝ month at a time. Each entry keeps the Church's day it was written on (the feast, the fast, that day's ግጻዌ), so an old entry reads as more than a date. Entries can be locked behind a passphrase, and the journal never appears in screenshots or the app switcher.
  • አስራት and ስዕለት A ledger for the tithe: record what you receive and the tenth is worked out, record what you give and it shows what is still owed. Vows are tied to the feast they were promised on, setting what was promised against what has been kept.
  • ጉዞ, a record without punishment Track daily prayer and your own habits. There is no broken-streak language here; a missed day is a missed day.
  • Reminders that respect the night Prayer-time notifications with per-mode settings, a nightly nudge, a morning ግጻዌ reading, and quiet hours that silence all of them.
  • Search that forgives spelling Amharic search treating ሀ/ሐ/ኀ, ሰ/ሠ and ጸ/ፀ as equal, across the prayers, the Psalter, the New Testament, ስንክሳር and ውዳሴ ማርያም.
  • Bookmarks, highlights, sharing Colour any verse in one of four colours and it stays coloured everywhere it appears. Copy or share a verse, a reading or a chapter as text or as paginated image cards, and save them to the gallery.
  • Backup you choose the contents of Export is a checklist, not all-or-nothing. The journal is off by default and asks for your passphrase before it can be included; confession drafts are never exportable at all.
  • A home-screen widget Today's ምስባክ and ወንጌል, without opening anything.

Corrections
