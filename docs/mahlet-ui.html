Audit findingWhat the drawing does
Three orders unreachable, and 41 ≠ 38A ቀን ያልተወሰነላቸው section on the season screen. One fix closes both, because they are the same bug from two sides.
Nothing is searchableA search field at the top of the list, over feast names, days and part keys.
No way to change text sizeAn A in the order bar, the same control the Bible reader and ስንክሳር already have.
Twelve screenfuls with no way to jumpA contents button and an ፫ / ፳፪ readout with a progress line.
Editions read as continuationOne sentence above the pills, and a plain first label instead of እትም 0.
ወይም is one faint wordA gold rule down the alternative and a bordered ወይም tag, so 150 dp of substitute chant is visibly a substitute.
Chips look tappable and are notEach chip opens its own order. The row keeps its default.
Month strip never shows where you areThe current month is lit, and the strip fades at the edge to say it scrolls.
33 rows read identicallyThe feast name becomes the title; the month and part count become chips.

One thing deliberately not drawn. A day with no order still needs a sentence, and the string for it already exists and is unused. It is not in these frames because all three happen to fall on days that do have one, which is itself the point: roughly three days in four have nothing, and that is the state the screen has never been designed for.

Content is real: ገብረ መንፈስ ቅዱስ on ጥቅምት ፭ genuinely carries a 5-part ዋዜማ and a 22-part ማኅሌት with three editions, and the parts shown are its first four. The three undated ጽጌ orders are the ones with no route in today. Colours are the app's own hero green, arke red and gold. Nothing here is built yet.
