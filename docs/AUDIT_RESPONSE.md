## Architectural Principles Preserved

1. **Strict Offline Guarantee**: Absolutely zero network calls, zero analytics SDKs, and zero mandatory cloud services.
2. **Non-Punitive Devotion**: No streaks, no consecutive-day penalties, no guilt copy. A returning worshipper is met with peace and "Welcome back" (እንኳን ደህና መጡ).
3. **Content-Addressed Identity Contract**: All bookmarks, highlights, and journal entries are permanently keyed by content identity (`scripture:<edition>:<book>:<chapter>:<verse>`), guaranteeing that font resizing, orientation changes, and app migrations never break user bookmarks.
4. **Cultural & Typographic Fidelity**: Strict Ethiopic typography invariants (`letterSpacing = 0.sp`, `includeFontPadding = false`, red rubrication ቀይ ጽሑፍ, and classical Ge'ez numerals ፩-፼) are maintained everywhere.
