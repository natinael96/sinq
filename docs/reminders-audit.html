ስንቅ · reminders audit

What rings, and what does not

An audit of the prayer reminder modes, ሰዓታት አስተካክል and ልማዶች አስተካክል, read from the source. Everything below has a file and a line behind it. The first finding is the one that matters most.

A fresh install rings for no prayer hour at all

The built-in mode ships eight entries, one per hour, at 06:00, 09:00, 12:00, 15:00, 18:00, 21:00, 00:00 and 22:00. Every one of them is off. ReminderEntry.enabled defaults to false and builtInMode() never overrides it, so the eight exist as rows in an editor and nothing else. Nothing in onboarding turns any on.

Someone installing ስንቅ to be called to prayer was not called, and the app never said so. The Settings page reported reminders as on, because that count is built from the four daily switches and the ledgers, and it contains no prayer hour at all.

Fixed. The six day hours now ring from first launch. ንዋም at midnight and ሌሊት ፱ ሰዓት at ten do not — those are taken up, not assumed, and an app that wakes a stranger at midnight on the day they install it has misread what it was asked for. Because a changed default cannot reach anyone already installed, the page now says outright when no hour will ring, with a way straight to the switches.

And one of the eight could not be turned on where it stood. ሌሊት ፱ ሰዓት shipped hidden — HoursConfig(hidden = setOf("veil")) — and a hidden hour is filtered out of the mode editor and out of the scheduler. Its 22:00 entry was stored, counted, and unreachable until the reader found ሰዓታት አስተካክል, two screens away under a different heading. It no longer ships hidden.

1How a reminder actually travels

One alarm is armed at a time per entry. When it fires it re-arms the next one before deciding whether to ring — that chain is what keeps tomorrow alive, and it is also where the losses hide.

2Three screens, three stores, one look

5What I would do next, in order

  1. Put the hours and their times together. One screen: the hour, whether it is shown, and what time it rings. Today, making an hour exist lives under ጸሎት and setting the time it rings lives under ማንቂያዎች, four taps apart under unrelated headings. This is the deepest confusion left here and the one real design change still outstanding.
  2. Either wire up habit cadence or stop offering it. Setting a schedule for ንባብ, or for any habit you add yourself, does nothing. Only ስንክሳር, ቤተ ክርስቲያን and ስግደት ever reach the nightly note.
  3. Stop counting reminders that cannot ring. An inactive mode still reports "N reminders on", and a reminder pointing at a hidden hour is still counted though the editor will not show it.
  4. Ask before deleting a custom hour or habit. Deleting a mode asks. These two do not, and both orphan the records and reminders that referenced them.
  5. Leave a trace when quiet hours swallow a prayer. The alarm is suppressed and the "done?" prompt never appears, so the hour is invisible in every record, indistinguishable from one that was never set.
