## 2. Exact Alarms Permission (`USE_EXACT_ALARM`) Declaration

When submitting on Google Play Console, go to **App content > Exact alarms**.

- **Question:** Does your app meet the acceptable use cases for `USE_EXACT_ALARM`?
- **Selection:** **Alarm clock or timer** (or Reminder app for core liturgical times).
- **Core Use Case Explanation (Paste this into the form):**
> "Sinq is a liturgical prayer book and canonical hour alarm application for the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo tradition. The app's core functionality requires sounding exact prayer alarms at predetermined canonical hours throughout the day and night (such as 06:00 Morning, 09:00 Terce, 12:00 Sext, 15:00 None, 18:00 Vespers, 21:00 Compline, and 00:00 Midnight). Because users rely on the app to wake them or notify them for specific liturgical devotions, inexact alarms are inadequate and lead to missed communal prayer times. The app uses AlarmManager.setAlarmClock() to guarantee on-time delivery across device sleep and Doze modes."
- **Video / Evidence Link:**
> Provide a short 30-second screen recording showing the Home screen's prayer reminder configuration, setting an alarm time, and showing the alarm firing.
