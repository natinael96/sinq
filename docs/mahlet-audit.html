Three orders cannot be opened at all

Month 0 — the bucket for orders with no fixed day — holds five orders. Three of them carry season = "tsige": the seven-year ማኅሌተ ጽጌ አቋቋም, and the ጥቅምት and ኅዳር ones. Forty-seven parts of chant.

The list screen filters season != TSIGE out of its dated months and out of its ቀን ያልተወሰነላቸው section, on the reasoning that ጽጌ has a door of its own. That door, the season screen, reads only months 1, 2 and 3. Month 0 is in neither. There is no path to these three from anywhere in the app.

The same split shows on the surface as a number that disagrees with itself: the ዘመነ ጽጌ row counts all 41 ጽጌ orders in the index, and the screen it opens says ፴፰. One tap apart, same object, two counts, and the difference is exactly the three that cannot be reached.

1What a reader can and cannot do with the text

ማኅሌት is the longest continuous reading in the app — a median order runs about five screenfuls and the longest, መድኃኔዓለም, runs twelve. It is also the least equipped reader in the app.

The font case is the sharp one. The order screen reads the reader's chosen text size and applies it to the chant, but offers no way to change it. To make ማኅሌት bigger you must leave, open the Bible or ስንክሳር, change it there, and come back. It is the only long-form reader in the app that takes the setting without offering the control.

2Half the corpus has an alternative, and the screen barely says so

An edition is a whole other text of the same order, read instead of the book's, never after it. That distinction is the reason the merged corpus exists.

What the screen offers is a strip of pills. Tapping one swaps the whole text. Nothing anywhere says these are the same order in different recensions rather than different parts of it, there is no way to compare two, and switching loses your place and your selection. The first pill is labelled from whichever of three different things the order happens to carry, so it reads እትም 0, ቴሌግራም or ማኅሌተ ጽጌ አቋቋም depending. And እትም 0 uses an ASCII zero while every sibling uses Ge'ez numerals, which have no zero.

4Faults worth fixing on the rows themselves

WhereWhat happens
Feast rowsThe ዋዜማ and ነግሥ chips look tappable and are not. The row always opens the ዋዜማ, so on the 50 feasts that have both, a reader wanting the morning service always lands on last night's and must find the tab.
Feast rowsThe chip line cannot wrap or ellipsize. On 19 groups it runs past the edge of a narrow phone and is cut mid-word.
Season screenThirty-three rows in a run, each titled with the month and subtitled with a part count. The feast name is never drawn, so they read identically and are told apart only by a numeral.
Order screenTelegram reference rows are 22 dp tall, under half the minimum tap target, and leave the app without warning.
Order screenThe gold line under the title carries raw Telegram post titles, up to 64 characters with inconsistent spelling, truncated to about 38.
Order screenAn unknown order id renders a blank title and the words " ክፍል" with a leading space, because the Ge'ez numeral for zero is empty.
All threeNo empty state anywhere. A failed load draws a blank screen.
List screenAlone among these screens it uses a bare scrolling column, so it has no scroll indicator and no maximum width — on a tablet it stretches edge to edge.

5What I would do, in order

  1. Give the three stranded orders a route. Either let the season screen read month 0, or stop filtering season orders out of the undated section. Either fixes the count mismatch at the same time, because both are the same bug seen from two sides.
  2. Put ማኅሌት in search. It is the only large corpus in the app you cannot type your way into, and feast names are exactly what a reader knows.
  3. Give the order screen the reader's toolbar. Font sizing above all, since it already obeys the setting, plus a contents sheet — twelve screenfuls with no way to jump is the single worst reading experience in the app.
  4. Say what an edition is. One line above the pills naming them as alternative texts of the same order would do most of the work, and the first pill needs one honest label rather than three.
  5. Make the feast chips real. If a row shows ዋዜማ and ነግሥ, tapping either should open that one.
  6. Say when a day has nothing. The string already exists.
  7. Show the feast name on the season rows. Thirty-three identical rows is not a list, it is a wall.
