What changed

The most recent releases, in short. Each version's full notes, including why a bug happened, are in the changelog.

1.6.1 31 August 2026

the journal keeps what you wrote

Fixed

  • Journal entries could be lost when the editor closed. Nothing was written to disk until you left the screen, so switching apps at the wrong moment left no record at all. Entries now save a second after you stop typing, and a save that starts always finishes.

Changed

  • The journal says when it has saved. A tick appears in the entry header once the text is on disk. There is still no Save button, but “is this saved?” now has an answer.

1.6.0 31 August 2026

what is owed, what was promised, what was thought

Added

  • አስራት. A ledger for the tithe: record what you receive and the tenth is worked out for you; record what you give and it shows what is still owed, over an Ethiopian month or year. The share is adjustable, amounts are counted in a currency you name, and giving beyond the tithe is shown as a surplus rather than a negative debt.
  • ስዕለት. Vows and pledges, each tied to the feast it was promised on, with what was promised set against what has been kept. A one-time vow stops reminding once it is fulfilled; a standing one keeps its rhythm.
  • ማስታወሻ, a journal. Write the day down, browsed a ግእዝ month at a time. Every entry keeps the Church's day it was written on (the feast, the fast, that day's ግጻዌ), so an old entry reads as more than a date. Entries can be locked behind a passphrase, and the journal never appears in screenshots or the app switcher.
  • Preparing for ንስሐ. A kind of entry built to be destroyed: it never leaves the device, never enters a backup, and “ንስሐ ገብቻለሁ” deletes it rather than filing it away.
  • Feast-anchored reminders. A reminder can now recur on a yearly date or a named feast. The ወርኀዊ በዓላት come from the bundled ስንክሳር itself, and movable feasts such as ፋሲካ are computed each year from the ባሕረ ሓሳብ.
  • Choose what a backup contains. Export is now a checklist rather than all-or-nothing. The journal is off by default and asks for your passphrase before it can be included.

Changed

  • Quiet hours now silence every reminder. They previously applied only to the ringing prayer alarms; the nightly, ግጻዌ, ምጽዋት and ንስሐ notifications ignored them. Because the silence is now total, Settings warns when a reminder is timed inside the window and would never arrive.
  • The Reminders page is grouped (daily, giving, and sound) instead of one flat run of rows.

1.5.2 31 August 2026

the alarm asks again

Fixed

  • “ጨርሰዋል?” comes back after an alarm. The follow-up used to appear only if you swiped the notification away, not after Dismiss, Open, or the timeout. Every ending now asks, except Snooze.

Changed

  • The ጉዞ page is more compact: a tighter heading, hero and section rhythm, so more of the day's habits sit on screen.

1.5.1 31 August 2026

the home page, restored

Fixed

  • The daily-psalms and ጸሎት ዘዘወትር cards are back on the home page. A fix for clipped text at large sizes had let the cards grow into the whole page and push the bottom two off a dashboard that does not scroll.
  • The streak grid grows with its card instead of sitting adrift in a half-empty box at large text sizes.

Changed

  • The ስንክሳር button says which day it opens (“የሐምሌ ፰ ስንክሳር”), so moving between days relabels it.
